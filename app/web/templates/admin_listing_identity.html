Stable security and listing identity

Verify legacy aliases before linking them to stable security/listing IDs. ISIN identifies a security; venue, symbol, monetary currency and quote-unit scale identify its listing. Multiple aliases can share a listing without combining their holdings or events.

This is the identity preparation stage. Links are audited and reversible; catalogue metadata and financial records stay in place. Pricing and valuation still use the existing compatibility paths. Source metadata changes mark links stale and require fresh verification.
