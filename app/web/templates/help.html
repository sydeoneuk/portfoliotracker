❓ What is this app?

The Trading 212 Portfolio Tracker is a personal dashboard that connects to your Trading 212 account(s) via their official API and gives you a richer, more analytical view of your investments than the Trading 212 app itself provides.

It pulls in your positions, order history, dividend payments, and cash balances, then enriches that data with live market prices and additional company information to give you consolidated performance metrics across your ISA and Trading accounts in one place.

Note: This is a technology demonstration provided for informational purposes only. It is not financial advice and no liability is accepted for any errors in the data presented. Always verify important figures directly in the Trading 212 app.
🚀 Getting started

To connect the app to your Trading 212 account you need to generate API keys inside Trading 212 and paste them into Settings. You can connect a Trading account, an ISA account, or both.

Step-by-step
1
Log in to Trading 212 on the web or mobile app and go to Settings → API.
2
Create a new API key. Give it a descriptive name (e.g. "Portfolio Tracker"). Copy the key — you won't be able to see it again.
3
Open Settings in this app and paste the key into the appropriate field (Trading account or ISA). Repeat if you have both account types.
4
Click Run Sync Now in the Data Sync card. The first sync may take a minute or two depending on how many transactions you have.
5
Once the sync completes, navigate to Portfolio to see your holdings.
Tip: API keys are stored encrypted in the database — they are never stored in plain text.
Tip: If you only have one account type (e.g. just an ISA), you only need to enter that key. Leave the other field blank.
📊 Portfolio page

The Portfolio page is your main dashboard — it gives you a snapshot of your entire portfolio at the current moment.

Summary cards
  • Total Value — the current market value of all your holdings converted to GBP.
  • Total Cost — how much you originally paid for your current holdings (cost basis).
  • Total P&L — unrealised profit or loss: current value minus cost basis.
  • Total Return % — P&L expressed as a percentage of your cost basis.
  • Free Cash / Pie Cash — uninvested cash sitting in your accounts, shown if non-zero.
Holdings table

Each row represents one stock or ETF you currently hold. Columns include:

  • Ticker / Name — click any row to open the detailed Holding page for that instrument.
  • Account — ISA or Trading.
  • Quantity — shares held (can be fractional).
  • Avg Cost — your average purchase price per share in GBP.
  • Current Price — latest price fetched from Yahoo Finance, in GBP.
  • Value (GBP) — quantity × current price.
  • P&L (GBP) / % — unrealised gain or loss for this holding.
Pie filter

If you use Trading 212 Pies, a filter bar at the top of the table lets you narrow down holdings to a specific pie or view all pies together.

Tip: Prices are fetched live from Yahoo Finance each time the page loads. If a price looks stale or unusual, try a manual sync from Settings.
🔄 Transactions page

The Transactions page shows a complete history of every buy and sell order that has been executed across your accounts.

Summary cards
  • Total Bought — cumulative GBP value of all BUY orders.
  • Total Sold — cumulative GBP value of all SELL orders.
  • Net Invested — total bought minus total sold: the net capital you have deployed.
  • Total Transactions — count of all orders in the table.
Transactions table

Columns include the date, ticker, account, side (BUY/SELL), order type, quantity, fill price per share, and total value — all in GBP.

T212 cash fund transactions

By default, internal Trading 212 cash fund movements are hidden. Click Show T212 cash fund transactions to include them if you want to see a fully complete history.

Tip: Use the Filter box above the table to quickly search by ticker, name, or account. The table is fully sortable — click any column header.
Tip: Click any row to jump straight to that stock's Holding page.
💰 Dividends page

The Dividends page shows all dividend and income payments received across your accounts.

Filtering
  • Account buttons (All / ISA / Trading) — filter by which account received the dividend.
  • Month dropdown — filter to a specific calendar month to see income for that period.
  • Combine both filters to see, for example, ISA dividends in March 2025.
  • Click ✕ Clear filters to return to the full list.
Summary cards

The total GBP dividend income and payment count update automatically to reflect the active filters.

Dividends table

Shows each payment with its date, ticker, account, payment type, quantity of shares held at the time, per-share gross amount (in the instrument's native currency), and the total GBP amount received.

Tip: Per-share amounts are shown in the instrument's own currency (USD, EUR, GBP, etc.) so you can easily cross-reference with published dividend announcements. The Amount (GBP) column always shows what you actually received after Trading 212 converted it.
Tip: Use the month filter at year-end to add up your annual dividend income for ISA allowance or tax reporting purposes.
📈 Analysis page

The Analysis page provides visual breakdowns of your portfolio composition to help you understand where your money is concentrated.

Charts included
  • Allocation by Holding — a pie/donut chart showing each stock's share of your total portfolio value. Useful for spotting over-concentration.
  • Allocation by Sector — groups your holdings by industry sector (Technology, Financials, Healthcare, etc.).
  • Allocation by Geography / Country — shows how your portfolio is split across different countries and regions.
  • Account Split — compares the value held in your ISA versus your Trading account.
Pie filter

As with the Portfolio page, you can filter charts to show only the holdings within a specific Trading 212 Pie.

Tip: Sector and country data is sourced from Yahoo Finance. For some smaller or non-US stocks this data may occasionally be missing or approximate.
Tip: Use the geography chart to check whether your portfolio is overly concentrated in one market — a common risk for UK-based investors who hold mostly US tech.
📌 Individual Holding page

Clicking any ticker or row across the app takes you to the Holding page for that instrument — a deep-dive into a single stock or ETF.

What you'll find
  • Summary cards — current value, total cost, unrealised P&L, return %, average cost per share, and total shares held.
  • Company info — sector, industry, country, and a brief business description sourced from Yahoo Finance.
  • Order history — every buy and sell for this instrument, with dates, quantities, prices, and values.
  • Dividend history — all dividend payments received for this stock, with per-share amounts and totals.
Tip: The holding page is the best place to review the full cost basis of a position and understand your entry points over time.
🔄 Syncing your data

Data is not updated in real time — it is pulled from Trading 212 on demand when you trigger a sync.

How to sync

Go to Settings and click Run Sync Now in the Data Sync card. The page will refresh every few seconds and show the sync status. Once complete it will show the time of the last successful sync.

What gets synced
  • Current positions and quantities from both accounts
  • Full order (buy/sell) history
  • All dividend payment records
  • Account cash balances (free cash and cash held in Pies)
  • Pie structure and membership
  • Company metadata (sector, country, description) via Yahoo Finance
Live prices

Current market prices are fetched live from Yahoo Finance each time you load a page — they are not stored and do not require a sync. This means your portfolio value is always as up to date as Yahoo Finance's data allows.

Tip: Run a sync after making trades in Trading 212 to ensure your order history and position quantities are up to date.
Tip: The "Last synced" timestamp in the top bar tells you when the most recent successful sync completed. If it looks old, head to Settings to trigger a fresh one.
💡 Hints & tips
Search anywhere: Every table has a Filter box. Type a company name, ticker, or account type to instantly narrow results — no need to scroll through long lists.
Sort any column: Click any column header in the tables to sort ascending or descending. Hold Shift and click a second column to sort by multiple columns.
Navigate from tables: Clicking any row in the Portfolio, Transactions, or Dividends tables takes you directly to that holding's detail page.
Both accounts in one view: If you hold the same stock in both your ISA and Trading account they will appear as separate rows, letting you track the cost basis and P&L for each independently.
Currency handling: All values are normalised to GBP. Stocks traded in USD, EUR, or GBX (pence) are automatically converted using live FX rates.
Fractional shares: Quantities are displayed to 4 decimal places so fractional share positions (common with Trading 212 Pies) are shown accurately.
ISA allowance tracking: Filter the Dividends page to the current tax year months to keep track of income received inside your ISA.
Change page size: The "Show ___ transactions" dropdown at the bottom of each table lets you display 25, 50, 100, or all rows at once.
❓ Frequently asked questions
Why does a price look wrong or show as zero?

Prices come from Yahoo Finance and are occasionally unavailable for less common tickers, or for stocks that have been delisted. Check the ticker directly on Yahoo Finance. Running a sync will also attempt to refresh company metadata which may resolve lookup issues.

Why is my portfolio value different from the Trading 212 app?

Prices are sourced from Yahoo Finance rather than Trading 212's own feed, and are subject to the normal 15-minute delay for most exchanges. Small discrepancies due to FX rates and price timing are normal. This app is for informational analysis — always rely on the Trading 212 app for definitive account values.

My sector or country shows as "Unknown" — why?

Yahoo Finance does not always carry sector or country data for non-US listed stocks. Where possible the app falls back to a mapping based on the exchange the stock trades on (e.g. LSE → United Kingdom). If it still shows as Unknown, the data is genuinely unavailable from our sources.

Can I connect both an ISA and a Trading account?

Yes — enter API keys for both in Settings. Data from both accounts will be merged into a single view, with each holding labelled by account type.

Is my API key safe?

API keys are encrypted before being stored in the database using AES encryption. They are never logged or transmitted anywhere other than directly to the Trading 212 API. That said, treat your API key like a password — if you suspect it has been compromised, revoke it in Trading 212 immediately and generate a new one.

How do I remove my data?

Go to Settings. You can either Clear Portfolio Data (removes all synced trading data but keeps your account) or Delete Account (permanently removes everything including your login).

Why are T212 cash fund transactions hidden by default?

Trading 212 routes uninvested cash through an internal money market instrument. These show up as BUY/SELL orders but are not real equity investments. Hiding them by default keeps the transaction history focused on your actual stock trades.
