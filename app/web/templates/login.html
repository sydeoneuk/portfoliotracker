Trading 212 Portfolio

Your Trading 212 portfolio,
beautifully analysed.

Connect your Trading 212 account(s) via API and get a richer view of your investments — performance, income, allocation, and history — all in one place.

  • 📊
    Portfolio DashboardLive P&L, cost basis, and current value across your ISA and Trading accounts.
  • 💰
    Dividend TrackingFull income history with per-share amounts, currency conversion, and month-by-month filtering.
  • 📈
    Portfolio AnalysisVisual breakdowns by holding, sector, geography, and account type.
  • 🔄
    Complete Trade HistoryEvery buy and sell order, searchable and sortable, with GBP-normalised values.
